## Work on the repository

Begin at [start here](/developer/start-here) for access, pinned prerequisites, commands and troubleshooting. Recover the current scope through the [agent handoff](/developer/agent-handoff); Claude uses the same authority through [its entry guide](/developer/claude-handoff). The [provider-neutral workflow](https://github.com/Mindclade-org/mindclade-internal-monorepo/blob/main/.agents/workflows/foundation-development.md) separates criteria, implementation, review and integration. A reading guide or prompt grants no permission to execute a pending task. Runbooks cover [local verification](/runbooks/local-verification), [regenerating generated outputs](/runbooks/regenerate-generated-outputs) and [running Foundation Echo](/runbooks/run-foundation-echo); the [runbooks index](/runbooks/overview) also routes to the operator runbook and troubleshooting. Each gate's criteria and their status are indexed in [gate criteria](/superpowers/specs/overview).

Detailed procedures live in [supervised development](/developer/supervised-development), the [engineering packet contract](/developer/work-packet-v1) and [governance tool interfaces](/developer/governance-tools). Follow the [master readiness plan](/superpowers/plans/2026-09-23-v1-integration-readiness) for dependency order; these references do not duplicate its open task list.

## Operate and evaluate

The [GitHub governance runbook](/operations/github-governance) owns protected operation and the aftermath of Rob's manual Enterprise migration. [Governance status](/architecture/engineering/governance-status) records observed blockers. The [delivery and operations foundation](/architecture/foundations/delivery-and-operations) explains future platform operations and the evidence needed before deployment. No production deployment procedure is established by the planned infrastructure.

## Interpret records correctly

Accepted [ADRs](/architecture/adr/adr-000-mindclade-vnext-architecture-constitution) remain normative. The [original supplied blueprint](/architecture/sources/original-greenfield-blueprint) and dated reviews retain historical observations; active explanations reconcile them with accepted decisions. Files explicitly marked as blueprint scaffold stubs, including reserved generated architecture views, are planned placeholders. They are neither usable configurations nor Codegen-emitted artifacts.

Each document type (component README, runbook, API reference, guide, handoff, agent prompt or evaluation, and new criteria, reviews and ADRs) follows one template, listed in [document types and templates](https://github.com/Mindclade-org/mindclade-internal-monorepo/blob/main/.agents/workflows/architecture-docs.md#document-types-and-templates). `just verify-tree` checks every Markdown anchor, the required sections of component, runbook and API pages, and the preamble of agent prompts and evaluations.

This index replaces only its own original stub through the reviewed scaffold manifest transition. Read next: [foundations](/architecture/foundations/overview) for the design, or [start here](/developer/start-here) for contribution.
