ADR-002 establishes Codegen as the sole semantic compiler for Mindclade. Inputs such as TypeSpec, Protobuf, and domain contracts normalize into `SemanticInput@v1`, and all downstream outputs are reproducible projections.

| Status | Date | Related |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| Accepted | 2026-09-22 | Not stated |

## Context

Multiple input formats must be reduced to a single canonical semantic representation so that projections remain consistent and verifiable.

## Decision

Codegen is the sole semantic compiler. TypeSpec, Protobuf, domain contracts, and service descriptors are frontend inputs that normalize into `SemanticInput@v1`.

## Consequences

- OpenAPI, SDK, MCP, docs, Terraform, CDK, C ABI, and architecture output are projections.
- No downstream generator may reinterpret contract meaning independently.
- Semantic compatibility compares graph snapshots, not rendered projections.
