This page records ADR-005, the law governing how evidence, claims, policy, and decisions are modeled and separated. It is authoritative: architecture-law changes require explicit ADR review.

## Status

| Attribute | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Status | Accepted |
| Date | 2026-09-22 |

## Context

Without clear separation, observed data, assertions, rules, and outcomes can collapse into a single undifferentiated primitive, making qualification and review impossible.

## Decision

Evidence, claims, policies, and decisions are separate primitives.

Evidence is observed proof material. A claim is an assertion about a subject. A policy determines whether evidence supports the claim. A decision records the outcome.

## Consequences

- A benchmark result is evidence, not a scientific claim.
- A passing test is evidence, not release qualification.
- Human approval and automated qualification share the same decision model.
