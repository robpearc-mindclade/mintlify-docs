## Decision

Agents are governed capability executors. They receive explicit task scope, owned paths, budgets, tools, credentials, network policy, and provenance requirements.

## Consequences

- No ambient authority.
- No automatic privilege inheritance by child agents.
- Coding agents may implement, but claims require independent evidence and policy decisions.
