## Context

Before any service, model, or evidence can be trusted, the system must have a single law for what an artifact is and how it is addressed.

## Decision

`Identity`, `Reference<T>`, and `Artifact` are foundational primitives. References are typed and immutable. Artifacts carry identity, digest, kind, and provenance.

## Consequences

- Datasets, checkpoints, models, generated outputs, deployment plans, evidence bundles, and scientific outputs share one identity law.
- Digest and provenance are required for trust.
