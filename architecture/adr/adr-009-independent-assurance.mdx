This architecture decision record establishes that assurance must be independent from implementation. Conformance, compatibility, security, replay, numerical, scientific, release, and deployment qualification all consume artifacts and evidence. The implementation owner does not define success and self-certify it.

| Status | Date | Related |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Accepted | 2026-09-22 | — |

## Decision

Assurance must be independent from implementation.

## Consequences

- Agent-generated work requires separate review or evaluator ownership.
- Scientific and numerical claims require evidence beyond source review.
- Local source checks are not remote CI, deployment, or release claims.
