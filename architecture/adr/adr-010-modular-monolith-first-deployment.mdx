This architecture decision record records the choice to start with a modular monolith. Distribution into separately deployed services requires deployment and operational evidence first.

| Status | Date | Related |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Accepted | 2026-09-22 | — |

## Decision

Mindclade starts modular-monolith-first. Service boundaries must be semantically clear before they become separately deployed services.

## Consequences

- Domain ownership is enforced in source and graph metadata.
- Distribution requires deployment and operational evidence.
- Services are deployable wrappers around capabilities, RuntimeKit, generated transports, and infrastructure ports.
