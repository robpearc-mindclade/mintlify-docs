## Context

Without a canonical authority, biological data and claims fragment across ingestion pipelines, learned models, and assays, making reproducibility and traceability impossibleWithout a canonical authority, biological data and claims fragment across ingestion pipelines, learned models, and assays, making reproducibility and traceability impossible.

## Decision

Mindclade will maintain canonical biological semantics rather than letting ingestion, models, evaluation, and experiments invent competing meanings.

## Consequences

- Biological entities, sequences, structures, measurements, assays, cells, organisms, and relationships receive stable identities.
- Scientific claims must reference canonical biological meaning and supporting evidence.
- VS-001 records this law but does not implement the biology domain yet.
