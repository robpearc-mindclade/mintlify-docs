ADR-012 governs deployment mutation through a staged path. Every deployment must pass through authoring, synthesis, deployment planning, qualification, signing, promotion, GitOps, and reconciliation before it becomes live authority.

| Status | Date | Related |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| Accepted | 2026-09-22 | VS-001 |

## Context

Without a governed pipeline, infrastructure-as-code and synthesized plans can become live mutation authority without adequate review, evidence, or traceability.

## Decision

Deployment mutation is governed through a staged path:

```text
authoring -> synthesis -> DeploymentPlan -> qualification -> signing -> promotion -> GitOps -> reconciliation
```

## Consequences

- CDK and Terraform synthesize plans but do not serve as live mutation authority.
- Deployment claims require signed artifacts, policy decisions, and reconciliation evidence.
- VS-001 records the path without deploying anything.
