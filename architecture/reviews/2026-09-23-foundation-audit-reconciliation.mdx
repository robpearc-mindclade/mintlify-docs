## Plan change

The [written design](/superpowers/specs/2026-09-23-vs001-durable-capability-design) and [delivery plan](/superpowers/plans/2026-09-23-vs001-delivery) now have **16 tasks** across four stages, with a new [B4 boundary-enforcement task](/superpowers/plans/2026-09-23-vs001-b-composition#task-b4-executable-architecture-and-dependency-enforcement).

B4 preserves YAML authority and Codegen's sole semantic-compiler role. Structured Cargo/Bazel/TypeScript facts are checked against compiled rules. Negative fixtures cover public-to-internal imports, transitive re-exports, RuntimeKit-to-service/test-only dependencies, core layering, unknown ownership, cycles, forbidden dependencies, omitted configurations, missing collectors and stale provenance. The named validator wrappers must invoke real checks and propagate failure rather than become successful stubs.

C5 and D3 rerun B4 after persistence and assessment dependencies are introduced. Independent GitHub identities, protected checks and external trust custody remain distinct requirements. The competing JSON architecture/ownership/dependency placeholders remain inactive.
