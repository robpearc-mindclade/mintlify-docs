## Remaining acceptance loop

The [runbook](https://github.com/Mindclade-org/mindclade-internal-monorepo/blob/main/docs/operations/github-governance.md) defines the gated actions. All of the following remain required before declaring supervised readiness:

On a resumed task, first reconcile the tracked [handoff checkpoint](https://github.com/Mindclade-org/mindclade-internal-monorepo/blob/main/docs/developer/agent-handoff.md#current-task-register) with actual ownership, source, and retained receipts. That record identifies which parts of the remaining loop have subsequently acquired evidence. Neither conversation history nor ignored working notes are required authority for continuing.

1. Parent completes admission/audit/evidence integration and source verification for the actual combined candidate, including fresh task and whole-candidate review. Preserve failures and limitations; partial/historical receipts do not pass this gate.
2. Rob approves exact packet and immutable accepted-policy inputs, and separately selects trusted tool/evaluator provenance. The operator records approvals and accepted TOOL_ROOT outside candidate control. Authoring policy is not bootstrap.
3. Resolve destination SAML, entitlement, permissions, name, and private inherited audience. Obtain specific transfer authorization, pause effects, inventory, and verify the same repository and its integrations after transfer.
4. Configure and verify the dedicated App and effective main protections. Prove protected-path denials and merge-group delivery on the authorized disposable probe before activating the target one-change, human-admitted queue.
5. Complete the actual Linear-linked protected PR with exact candidate checks, fresh review, resolved threads, and Rob approval. Observe the real queue candidate/base and final main independently. Re-admit changed inputs.
6. After main verification, obtain Rob's approval for the particular private archive, verify immutability/audience/tag protections, publish without latest product-release status, download and compare with operator-held digests and identities. Then Rob accepts Linear closure; Notion receives outcome links.

A governance loop does not clear B4-dependent code admission, complete Echo, adopt a vendor, or pass the roadmap's independent scientific/release/runtime and deployment gates. Missing evidence keeps the relevant lane BLOCKED.
