This page explains the full Mindclade target system, how its parts relate, and what evidence each part needs. It is an authored design reference dated 2026-09-23. The repository contains a partial compiler and kernel foundation; the wider platform, runtime, scientific planes, and deployment fabric remain planned. These pages explain accepted intent. They do not activate scaffolded packages, define new wire schemas, authorize execution, or establish qualification.

Use the [current task register](/developer/agent-handoff#current-task-register) for remaining work and the [maturity model](https://github.com/Mindclade-org/mindclade-internal-monorepo/blob/main/docs/architecture/maturity.yaml) for recorded scope.

## One architecture, several useful views

The layered [platform diagram](/architecture/platform-diagram) shows semantic compilation, platform/execution, and shared laws/delivery. The [system design](/architecture/system-design) provides a continuous overview. The [master blueprint](/architecture/master-blueprint) organizes the same design into responsibilities and ownership. The four responsibilities (stable truth, dynamic execution, independent assurance, and governed evolution) apply across the layers; they are not four competing systems.

One Rust Codegen compiler seals an immutable `MindcladeGraph`. Semantic and Capability views borrow that graph; their names do not create separate editable authorities. Services compose domain capabilities and shared platform mechanisms. Logical planes do not require separately deployed microservices. External products remain adapters around Mindclade-owned meaning and policy.

The image is a reference illustration, not a Codegen-emitted atlas or a live topology. The complete [repository tree](/architecture/repository-tree) is a mechanical inventory, not proof that every reserved path is implemented. Generated architecture models and views remain subject to their own compiler and evidence work; manually authored foundation pages do not substitute for them.

## Authority and maintenance

[AGENTS.md](https://github.com/Mindclade-org/mindclade-internal-monorepo/blob/main/AGENTS.md) defines precedence. Accepted ADRs and active architecture metadata govern these explanations. Exact schemas and behavior stay in their linked source contracts and specifications. The immutable [original blueprint](/architecture/sources/original-greenfield-blueprint) preserves the user's design input; [ADR-015](/architecture/adr/adr-015-original-blueprint-scaffold-alignment) records its scaffold exception and graph clarification.

Use [repository layout](/architecture/model/repository-layout) and [CODEOWNERS](https://github.com/Mindclade-org/mindclade-internal-monorepo/blob/main/CODEOWNERS) for boundary and review ownership. Use the [generated-output policy](/architecture/generated-output-policy) for regeneration and provenance. Change an authority first when a design decision changes, then update its explanatory references. Do not copy volatile task status into this package or rewrite historical evidence to appear current.
